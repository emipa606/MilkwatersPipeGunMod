# What is a pipe gun?

Pipe guns are identical to their normal counterparts, but they are cheaper and have a 15% chance to jam, cancelling the shot and ending their burst early. When I say counterpart I mean that each gun is based on a vanilla gun, same stats but just with a 15% chance to jam. So like the pipe revolver is based on the revolver, the pipe rifle is based on the bolt-action rifle, etc.

The only mechanically different weapon is the pipe LMG, which has a bayonet for stab damage. To balance this, its crafting cost is higher than other pipe guns.

Raiders will use them sometimes and you can craft them by going through the normal research tree.
